Pixel art sprite sheet of 3 hidden TRAPS for a medieval base-building strategy game, each sitting flat on a single map tile, seen from the same top-down 3/4 view as the game's soldiers (NOT isometric):
Column 1 "Bomb": a round black iron bomb half buried in a small patch of dug earth, a short twisted fuse on top, a riveted band around it.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheet): chibi pixel art, the same palette, outline and shading as the soldiers. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726), saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: 3 columns × 2 rows, every cell exactly 192×192 px, each trap centred and about 55% of the cell width (they are small: one map tile). Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground shadow outside the object, no glow, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
