# Lò nghiền (Grinder)

Mã công trình: `lo-nghien` · chân đế 2×2 ô · ô 384×384 px

**Gửi kèm ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png` (Kiếm sĩ) và sheet Thành trì (`sprite-pixels/thanh-tri.png`) để giữ nét vẽ, góc nhìn và cỡ. Gửi lại dạng **File** PNG.

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/lo-nghien.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite sheet of a MAGIC GRINDING MILL that crushes unwanted weapons and armour into glowing powder: a sturdy stone mill house with a big round millstone turned by a wooden wheel and gears, a wide funnel on top where old swords and helmets are thrown in, and at the front a spout pouring sparkling coloured powder into a wooden bucket; sacks of powder stacked beside it, a small blue banner.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheets): chibi pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, the same palette, outline and shading as the game's soldiers and the Town Hall. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726) around every object and between parts. Saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal and gold. Chunky, readable, toy-like shapes like Clash of Clans buildings drawn as pixel art. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the sprite. Team colour is royal BLUE (banners, flags) so it can be recoloured later.

CAMERA: the same top-down 3/4 view as the soldiers and the Town Hall (NOT isometric, NOT a diamond): the building faces the viewer (front at the bottom of the picture), we see its front and its top from above at about 45°. Its square footprint looks like a slightly flattened square, front edge horizontal.
The footprint is 2×2 map tiles.

LEVELS (one per column, the same building growing bigger and richer, same footprint and silhouette family; every level clearly more impressive than the previous one):
Column 1 "Level 1": a small wooden hut with a hand-cranked stone millstone, a rough wooden funnel, one bucket of grey powder.
Column 2 "Level 2": a stone base with a bigger millstone turned by a wooden water-wheel style crank, iron bands, two sacks of yellow and green powder.
Column 3 "Level 3": a grey stone mill house with a slate roof, a large wooden gear wheel on the side, a copper funnel full of broken swords, blue powder pouring out.
Column 4 "Level 4": stone walls with polished BRONZE gears and pipes, a glowing purple rune on the millstone, purple sparkles in the spout, an orange-glowing chimney.
Column 5 "Level 5": a grand mill of white stone and GOLD gears, a giant enchanted millstone with glowing red and gold runes (solid pixels), a gold funnel, red powder flowing like lava into a gold cauldron, blue-and-gold banners.
Column 6 "Ruin": the destroyed building: a low pile of broken stone, splintered wood and dust on the same footprint, nothing taller than one third of the level 1 building.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: 6 columns × 1 row, every cell exactly the same size (384×384 px), the building centred horizontally, the bottom of its footprint on the same baseline near the bottom of every cell, identical scale in every cell (level 5 may be slightly taller, never wider). The footprint is 2×2 map tiles: the building fills about 88 % of the cell width. Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground, no grass, no ground shadow, no glow, no halo, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
