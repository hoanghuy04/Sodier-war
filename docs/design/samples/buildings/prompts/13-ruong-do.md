# Rương đồ (Storage Chest)

Mã công trình: `ruong-do` · chân đế 2×2 ô · ô 320×320 px

**Gửi kèm ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png` (Kiếm sĩ) và sheet Thành trì (`sprite-pixels/thanh-tri.png`) để giữ nét vẽ, góc nhìn và cỡ. Gửi lại dạng **File** PNG.

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/ruong-do.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite sheet of a big TREASURE STORAGE building of the base where the hero keeps equipment: a small sturdy vault house whose front is mostly a huge iron-bound wooden chest with the lid half open showing glints of weapons, gold coins and gems inside, iron corner plates, a big padlock hanging open, a blue banner on top.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheets): chibi pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, the same palette, outline and shading as the game's soldiers and the Town Hall. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726) around every object and between parts. Saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal and gold. Chunky, readable, toy-like shapes like Clash of Clans buildings drawn as pixel art. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the sprite. Team colour is royal BLUE (banners, flags) so it can be recoloured later.

CAMERA: the same top-down 3/4 view as the soldiers and the Town Hall (NOT isometric, NOT a diamond): the building faces the viewer (front at the bottom of the picture), we see its front and its top from above at about 45°. Its square footprint looks like a slightly flattened square, front edge horizontal.
The footprint is 2×2 map tiles.

LEVELS (one per column, the same building growing bigger and richer, same footprint and silhouette family; every level clearly more impressive than the previous one):
Column 1 "Level 1": a large wooden chest with rope handles on a little wooden platform, a simple iron latch.
Column 2 "Level 2": a bigger chest with iron bands and corners on a stone platform, a padlock, a few gold coins spilling out.
Column 3 "Level 3": a small stone vault house with a big iron-bound chest as its front door, metal studs, gems glinting inside.
Column 4 "Level 4": a stone vault with polished BRONZE plates, two chests stacked, a glowing purple gem lock, weapons sticking out of the chests.
Column 5 "Level 5": a grand treasury of white stone and GOLD: a huge golden chest with jewels, overflowing gold and glowing items (solid pixels), gold lion statues on both sides, blue-and-gold banners, the most impressive version.
Column 6 "Ruin": the destroyed building: a low pile of broken stone, splintered wood and dust on the same footprint, nothing taller than one third of the level 1 building.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: 6 columns × 1 row, every cell exactly the same size (320×320 px), the building centred horizontally, the bottom of its footprint on the same baseline near the bottom of every cell, identical scale in every cell (level 5 may be slightly taller, never wider). The footprint is 2×2 map tiles: the building fills about 88 % of the cell width. Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground, no grass, no ground shadow, no glow, no halo, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
