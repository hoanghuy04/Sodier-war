# Hội thành (Guild Fortress)

Mã công trình: `hoi-thanh` · chân đế 3×3 ô · ô 384×384 px

**Gửi kèm ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png` (Kiếm sĩ đã duyệt) và sheet Thành trì (`sprite-pixels/thanh-tri.png`) để giữ đúng nét vẽ, góc nhìn và cỡ. Gửi lại dạng **File** PNG.

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/hoi-thanh.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite sheet of a GRAND WAR FORTRESS called the Guild Hall, the barracks where the player's elite troops wait to defend the base (like the Clan Castle in Clash of Clans, but darker and more imposing). It is built from BLACK BRICKS: big rectangular dark charcoal-black stone blocks laid one by one in clear staggered rows, every single brick visible with thin lighter mortar lines and a lighter top-left edge pixel, some bricks slightly chipped. Shape: a massive square fortress with thick battlemented walls (crenellations on top), a tall central donjon tower, two squat flanking towers, a huge arched iron-bound gate in the front wall with a raised portcullis showing a dark hall inside, glowing orange torches on both sides of the gate, arrow slits, a big royal BLUE war banner hanging over the gate with a white crossed-swords emblem, and a flag pole with a blue flag on the donjon. Imposing, heavy, menacing but heroic.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheets): chibi pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, the same palette, outline and shading as the game's soldiers and the Town Hall. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726) around every object and between parts. Saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal and gold. Chunky, readable shapes with slightly exaggerated proportions (sturdy, toy-like, like Clash of Clans buildings drawn as pixel art). Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the sprite. Team colour is royal BLUE (banners, flags) so it can be recoloured later.

CAMERA: the same top-down 3/4 view as the soldiers and the Town Hall (NOT isometric, NOT a diamond): the fortress faces the viewer (gate at the bottom of the picture), we see its front wall and its top from above at about 45°. Its square ground footprint looks like a slightly flattened square, front edge horizontal.
The footprint is 3×3 map tiles: the fortress fills about 88% of the cell width.

LEVELS (one per column, the same fortress growing bigger and more impressive, same footprint and the same overall silhouette so the player recognises it; every level is clearly more grand than the previous one):
Column 1 "Level 1": a small black-brick keep, low walls, a wooden gate with iron bands, one blue banner, simple crenellations.
Column 2 "Level 2": taller black-brick walls, iron portcullis, two flanking towers with dark slate roofs, torches by the gate.
Column 3 "Level 3": thicker walls with buttresses, the central donjon rises higher, iron spikes along the battlements, a big blue banner with the crossed-swords emblem.
Column 4 "Level 4": black bricks with polished BRONZE trims and rivets, horned bronze ornaments on the tower tops, red-glowing windows, chains on the gate, two big banners.
Column 5 "Level 5": the ultimate fortress: black bricks trimmed with GOLD, a gold crown-shaped top on the donjon, gold dragon-head gargoyles on the corners, glowing orange runes carved into some bricks (solid pixels), huge blue-and-gold banners, the most imposing silhouette.
Column 6 "Ruin": the destroyed fortress: a low pile of broken black bricks, a fallen banner, a bent portcullis and dust on the same footprint, nothing standing taller than one third of the level 1 building.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: 6 columns × 1 row, every cell exactly the same size (384×384 px), the building centred horizontally, the bottom of its footprint on the same baseline near the bottom of every cell, identical scale in every cell (level 5 may be slightly taller, never wider). Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground, no grass, no ground shadow, no glow, no halo, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
