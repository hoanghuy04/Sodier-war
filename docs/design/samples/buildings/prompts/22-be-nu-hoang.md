# Bệ Nữ hoàng (nơi Nữ hoàng Băng nghỉ / hồi máu)

Mã công trình: `be-nu-hoang` · chân đế 3×3 ô · ô 384×384 px · mở ở **Thành trì cấp 8** · 2 hình

Chỗ đứng của **Nữ hoàng Băng** (tướng giữ nhà mở ở Thành trì 8). Giống Bệ Vua: có địch vào vùng canh thì Nữ hoàng ra bắn, xong về; bị hạ thì hồi máu trên bệ.

**Gửi kèm ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png` (Kiếm sĩ đã duyệt) và sheet Thành trì (`sprite-pixels/thanh-tri.png`) để giữ đúng nét vẽ, góc nhìn và cỡ. Gửi lại dạng **File** PNG (không gửi dạng ảnh nén).

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/be-nu-hoang.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite of a FROST QUEEN'S ALTAR, the resting place of the base's ice queen: a round raised dais of pale blue-white marble with three curved steps on the front, in the middle an EMPTY elegant throne carved out of clear blue ICE with tall pointed crystal spikes forming a crown-like back, a graceful arch of ice crystals behind it, two tall slim lanterns with cold sky-blue flames at the front, frost patterns and small snow drifts on the steps, blue silk banners with a silver snowflake emblem. The throne is EMPTY (the queen is drawn separately and stands in front of it).

STYLE (match the attached reference sheets): chibi pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, the same palette, outline and shading as the game's soldiers and the Town Hall. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726) around every object and between parts. Saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal, gold and crystal. Chunky, readable shapes with slightly exaggerated proportions (cute, sturdy, toy-like base buildings drawn as pixel art). Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the sprite. Team colour is royal BLUE (banners, flags, cloth, painted plates) so it can be recoloured later.

CAMERA: the same top-down 3/4 view as the soldiers (NOT isometric, NOT a diamond): the building faces the viewer (front side at the bottom of the picture), we see its front side and its top from above at about 45°. Its square ground footprint looks like a slightly flattened square, front edge horizontal.
The footprint is 3×3 map tiles: the dais fills about 85% of the cell width; low and wide, the ice arch reaches about as high as the dais is wide.

LEVELS (one per column, the same building growing richer, same footprint and the same overall silhouette so the player recognises it):
Column 1 "Altar": the complete altar as described.
Column 2 "Ruin": the destroyed building: a low pile of broken stone, twisted metal, splintered beams and dust on the same footprint, nothing standing taller than one third of the level 1 building.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: 2 columns × 1 row, every cell exactly the same size (384×384 px), the building centred horizontally, the bottom of its footprint on the same baseline near the bottom of every cell, identical scale in every cell (level 3 may be slightly taller, never wider). Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground, no grass, no ground shadow, no glow on the background, no halo, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```

Công trình không có hướng (luôn quay mặt về phía người xem) và không cần hoạt ảnh nhiều khung: giàn giáo khi đang xây, đạn, tia và nổ do game tự vẽ.

Bệ không có cấp (cấp nằm ở Nữ hoàng). Chỉ 2 hình: nguyên vẹn + đổ nát.
