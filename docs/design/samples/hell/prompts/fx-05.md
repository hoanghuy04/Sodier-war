# Hiệu ứng: Phi Đao Bạc, Ngự Kiếm + tiến hóa

Sheet `fx-05` · 8 khung × 4 hàng · ô 256×256 px. Không vẽ nhân vật, chỉ hiệu ứng.

| Hàng | Kỹ năng / đòn | Tên hàng trong prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phi Đao Bạc | Silver Throwing Knife |
| 2 | Bão Đao Bạc (tiến hóa) | Silver Knife Storm |
| 3 | Ngự Kiếm | Flying Sword |
| 4 | Thiên Kiếm Trận (tiến hóa) | Heavenly Sword Formation |

**Gửi kèm:** `docs/design/samples/army/sprite-pixels/kiem-si.png` để khớp nét. Gửi lại dạng **File PNG**.

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/fx-05.png`

```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of silver knives and jade flying swords.
Row 1 "Silver Throwing Knife": frames 1-4: a small silver throwing knife flying to the right with a short white streak (projectile loop); frames 5-8: the hit, a small white star spark and two silver chips.
Row 2 "Silver Knife Storm": a spiral whirlwind of many small silver knives circling around the anchor just above the ground, short white streak trails, 8 frames of a seamless loop.
Row 3 "Flying Sword": frames 1-5: a jade-green glowing flying sword pointing right with a long jade trail (projectile loop); frames 6-8: it pierces, a jade slash spark.
Row 4 "Heavenly Sword Formation": a ring of 12 jade-green glowing swords floating in a circle above the anchor, points down: frames 1-3 the ring forms, frames 4-6 the swords strike down one after another with jade flashes on the ground ellipse, frames 7-8 the ring reforms.

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). These are the PLAYER'S skills on a dark red lava battlefield: use cool and bright colours (white, silver, gold, ice blue, violet, jade green) so they read clearly against red, orange and black.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 4 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: hàng có "loop" thì phát lặp; hàng hướng sang phải thì game xoay theo hướng bắn; vòng trên đất thì game co giãn theo bán kính kỹ năng.
