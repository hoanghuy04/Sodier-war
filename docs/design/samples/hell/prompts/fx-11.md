```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of map effects: lava geyser, fire wall, exit portal and the Hell Gate vortex.
Row 1 "Lava Geyser": frames 1-2: a bubbling vent with a red warning ring on the ground ellipse; frames 3-6: a tall column of lava erupts upward and splashes; frames 7-8: it falls back to bubbling.
Row 2 "Fire Wall": a short straight segment of a wall of tall roaring flames standing on the ground, filling the cell width (the game repeats it around the boss arena ring), 8 frames of a seamless loop.
Row 3 "Exit Portal": an upright round portal standing on the ground: a swirling ring of WHITE and GOLD light with a calm pale-blue centre, golden sparks rising, 8 frames of a seamless loop.
Row 4 "Hell Gate Vortex": a big swirling vortex of deep crimson and black with an orange fiery rim and floating embers, an upright oval seen from the front filling about 80% of the cell (it is laid over the arched opening of the Hell Gate building), 8 frames of a seamless loop.

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). Map effects: lava and fire are hot colours; the EXIT portal is the only white-gold and pale blue thing on the map, so the player finds it at once.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 4 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: hàng có "loop" thì phát lặp; hàng hướng sang phải thì game xoay theo hướng bắn; vòng trên đất thì game co giãn theo bán kính kỹ năng.
