```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of five player skills for a hell survival mode.
Row 1 "Purge Beam": a straight horizontal beam of white-gold holy light pointing to the RIGHT from the anchor, about 200 px long and 24 px thick, white-hot core with gold and pale-yellow bands, small light motes along it; frames 1-2 it grows out, frames 3-6 it flickers at full length (loop), frames 7-8 it thins and fades.
Row 2 "Fire Rune Mine": frames 1-4: a small round black iron mine lying on the ground with a glowing gold-orange rune on top that pulses brighter and dimmer (idle loop); frames 5-8: it explodes into a round burst of gold and orange flame with black iron shards, then a scorched ring on the ground ellipse.
Row 3 "Spirit Soldier": a translucent pale-blue ghost soldier with a helmet and spear, legs fading into a wisp tail, running to the RIGHT (frames 1-6 run loop), frames 7-8 it dissolves into blue wisps.
Row 4 "Ball Lightning": a ball of violet-white lightning about 70 px across with crackling arcs around it and small sparks (frames 1-6 loop, the arcs change every frame), frames 7-8 it pops into a burst of sparks.
Row 5 "Frost Nova": a white flash at the anchor, then a ring of ice-blue frost crystals and snow expanding outward on the ground ellipse (frames 2-6), then the ring leaves frozen shards that crack and fade (frames 7-8).

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). These are the PLAYER'S skills on a dark red lava battlefield: use cool and bright colours (white, silver, gold, ice blue, violet, jade green) so they read clearly against red, orange and black.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 5 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: hàng 1 game xoay theo góc quét; hàng 2 khung 1-4 lặp khi mìn nằm chờ, 5-8 khi nổ; hàng 3 game lật theo hướng chạy; hàng 4 lặp; hàng 5 co giãn theo bán kính.
