```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of four player skills (bronze mirror beam, paper crane, water wave, crossbow bolt) for a hell survival mode.
Row 1 "Demon Mirror Beam": a round bronze mirror with gold rim at the left of the cell, shining a cone of white-gold light to the RIGHT (cone about 60 degrees, 200 px long) with small floating glyph sparks inside; frames 1-2 the mirror glints and the cone grows, frames 3-6 the cone shimmers (loop), frames 7-8 it narrows and fades.
Row 2 "Paper Crane": frames 1-4: a white origami paper crane with a small gold glyph on its wing, about 60 px, gliding to the RIGHT with wings moving (loop); frames 5-8: it dives and explodes into a burst of white paper scraps and gold light on the ground ellipse.
Row 3 "Water Dragon Wave": a tall wide wall of clear blue and white water with a foamy crest shaped like a dragon head, seen from the side and moving to the RIGHT, about 120 px tall and 200 px wide; frames 1-2 it rises, frames 3-6 it rolls forward (loop, foam changes every frame), frames 7-8 it crashes down into a splash and foam on the ground.
Row 4 "Repeating Crossbow Bolt": frames 1-4: one thin silver crossbow bolt with a white-blue glowing tip and a short light streak behind it, flying to the RIGHT (loop, the streak flickers); frames 5-8: it hits and breaks into a small white star burst with silver splinters.

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks, puddles) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). These are the PLAYER'S skills on a dark red lava battlefield: use cool and bright colours (white, silver, gold, ice blue, violet, jade green) so they read clearly against red, orange and black; the water may be clear blue.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 4 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell. Leave a clear magenta gap between neighbouring cells.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: Hàng 1 game xoay theo hướng chiếu, co theo góc nón; hàng 2 khung 1-4 lặp khi bay, 5-8 khi lao xuống; hàng 3 lặp, game xoay theo hướng đi; hàng 4 khung 1-4 lặp khi bay, 5-8 khi trúng.
