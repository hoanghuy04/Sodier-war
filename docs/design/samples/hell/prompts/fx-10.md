```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of boss attacks: burrow eruption, lava wave, meteor, burning scales and a chain sweep.
Row 1 "Burrow Eruption": frames 1-3: a dark circle of cracked ground with a bright red warning rim; frames 4-6: the ground bursts upward in black rocks and lava; frames 7-8: debris falls back.
Row 2 "Lava Wave Cleave": a crescent wave of molten lava with a white-hot crest and black rocks travelling along the ground from the anchor to the right edge of the cell, breaking up at the end.
Row 3 "Lava Meteor": frames 1-3: a red warning circle on the ground ellipse and a black-and-orange meteor rock falling from the top; frame 4: impact; frames 5-8: a lava splash and a burning crater that fades.
Row 4 "Burning Scale": frames 1-4: ONE glowing burning ash scale (a small orange-rimmed grey leaf shape) spinning, centred (bullet loop); frames 5-8: it crumbles into sparks.
Row 5 "Chain Sweep": a giant heavy iron chain swinging in a wide horizontal arc around the anchor, close to the ground, red-hot sparks along the ground where it scrapes.

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). These are ENEMY attacks: use hot and toxic colours (crimson, orange, sulfur yellow, toxic green, black smoke); warning markers on the ground are bright red with a dark red rim.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 5 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: hàng có "loop" thì phát lặp; hàng hướng sang phải thì game xoay theo hướng bắn; vòng trên đất thì game co giãn theo bán kính kỹ năng.
