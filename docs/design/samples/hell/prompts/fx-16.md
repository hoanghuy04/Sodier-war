```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of four player passive effects (barrier bubble, dodge streak, frost hit, aura) for a hell survival mode.
Row 1 "Spirit Barrier": a round bubble shield around the anchor (about 150 px wide, its bottom on the ground ellipse), light blue and white with a thin gold hexagon pattern; frames 1-2 it forms, frames 3-6 it shimmers (loop), frames 7-8 it cracks and shatters into blue glass shards.
Row 2 "Mirage Step": a quick sideways whoosh: three pale violet-white afterimage streaks and small light motes moving to the RIGHT from the anchor, with a short "dodge" swirl of wind; frames 1-3 it appears, frames 4-8 it fades. No character shape, only streaks and motes.
Row 3 "Frost Bite": a small hit effect for one monster: frames 1-2 a white flash, frames 3-5 a cluster of ice-blue frost crystals forms over the anchor (about 90 px), frames 6-8 the crystals crack and fall as snow.
Row 4 "Killing Intent Aura": a flat aura on the ground ellipse around the anchor (about 160 px wide) made of sharp upward flame-like tongues of white and pale gold energy, with small rising sparks; frames 1-6 loop (the tongues change height every frame), frames 7-8 it fades. The game tints it red for Cuồng Nộ, so keep it mostly white and pale gold.

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks, puddles) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). These are the PLAYER'S skills on a dark red lava battlefield: use cool and bright colours (white, silver, gold, ice blue, violet, jade green) so they read clearly against red, orange and black.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 4 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell. Leave a clear magenta gap between neighbouring cells.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: Hàng 1 lặp quanh tướng, vỡ khi khiên hết; hàng 2 phát khi né; hàng 3 phát trên quái bị đóng băng; hàng 4 lặp dưới chân tướng (Sát Ý giữ màu, Cuồng Nộ game tô đỏ).
