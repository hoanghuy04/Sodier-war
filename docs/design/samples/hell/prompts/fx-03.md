# Hiệu ứng: vũ khí Hỏa Kỳ + tiến hóa, Lưỡi Liềm Xoay + tiến hóa

Sheet `fx-03` · 8 khung × 4 hàng · ô 256×256 px. Không vẽ nhân vật, chỉ hiệu ứng.

| Hàng | Kỹ năng / đòn | Tên hàng trong prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Roi Lửa | Fire Whip |
| 2 | Roi Long Hỏa (tiến hóa) | Fire Dragon Whip |
| 3 | Lưỡi Liềm Xoay | Orbiting Sickle |
| 4 | Bánh Xe Luân Hồi (tiến hóa) | Wheel of Rebirth |

**Gửi kèm:** `docs/design/samples/army/sprite-pixels/kiem-si.png` để khớp nét. Gửi lại dạng **File PNG**.

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/fx-03.png`

```text
Pixel art VFX sprite sheet of a fire whip, a small fire dragon, an orbiting sickle and a wheel of blades.
Row 1 "Fire Whip": a long fire whip lash cracking horizontally to the right: frames 1-2 the whip curls back, frames 3-4 a long S-shaped lash of red-orange flame with a white-hot core, frame 5 the tip cracks with a burst of sparks, frames 6-8 the flame line breaks into embers.
Row 2 "Fire Dragon Whip": a small serpentine fire dragon (orange-red flame body, white-hot core, yellow eyes, little horns) flying in a full circle around the anchor, 8 frames of a seamless loop, a short ember trail.
Row 3 "Orbiting Sickle": ONE silver sickle blade with a short white-blue trail, shown at 8 rotation steps for a seamless spin loop, centred in the cell (the game places several around the hero).
Row 4 "Wheel of Rebirth": a large flat silver wheel lying on the ground ellipse around the anchor, eight curved sickle blades as spokes, a ring of glowing white-gold ancient glyphs (abstract, not real letters), rotating: 8 frames of a seamless rotation loop.

STYLE: pixel art VISUAL EFFECT (VFX) sprite sheet for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, matching the attached reference sprite: same palette richness, chunky readable shapes, crisp hard pixel edges. Glows and light are drawn as hard-edged bands of 2-3 bright tones (white-hot core → bright colour → darker colour rim), like classic 16-bit game magic effects. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft airbrushed glow, NO semi-transparent pixels. Never use pink or magenta colours inside the effect. Anything lying on the ground (rings, circles, cracks) is seen from the 3/4 camera, so it is an ellipse about twice as wide as tall. Effects that travel or point in a direction point to the RIGHT (the game rotates them). These are the PLAYER'S skills on a dark red lava battlefield: use cool and bright colours (white, silver, gold, ice blue, violet, jade green) so they read clearly against red, orange and black. Exception: the two fire-whip rows of Hỏa Kỳ are red-orange fire, but always with a white-hot core.
LAYOUT: 8 columns × 4 rows. Each ROW is one animation, played left → right (column 1 = frame 1 … column 8 = frame 8), smooth step between frames. Every cell exactly 256×256 px, the effect centred on the SAME anchor point in every cell (the centre of the ground ellipse sits at the middle of the lower third of the cell), nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell.
BACKGROUND: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere, the effect pixels touch magenta directly. No characters, no hero, no monsters, no ground tiles, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no border.
```

Cách gắn vào game: hàng có "loop" thì phát lặp; hàng hướng sang phải thì game xoay theo hướng bắn; vòng trên đất thì game co giãn theo bán kính kỹ năng.
