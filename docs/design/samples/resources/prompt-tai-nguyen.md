# Mẫu vẽ 7 icon tài nguyên (thanh trên cùng)

Vẽ **1 ảnh 7 ô** (1 hàng), mỗi ô một tài nguyên, theo đúng thứ tự trên thanh tài nguyên của game. Icon hiện rất nhỏ (khoảng 20 px) nên hình phải đậm, rõ, ít chi tiết vụn.

**Gửi kèm ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png`. Lưu thành `resources/sprite-pixels/tai-nguyen.png`. Gửi dạng **File** (PNG) để nét nhất.

## Bước 1

```text
Pixel art icon sheet of 7 RESOURCE icons for the top resource bar of a strategy game, 7 columns × 1 row, one icon per cell, each a small pile or object seen at a 3/4 angle:
Cell 1 "Iron": two stacked steel-grey iron ingots with a cool blue sheen.
Cell 2 "Coal": a small heap of angular black coal lumps with blue-grey shiny faces.
Cell 3 "Gold": a shiny gold bar with two gold nuggets in front.
Cell 4 "Oil": a small wooden barrel with an amber-brown drop of crude oil above it.
Cell 5 "Tar": a thick drop of glossy black tar with a purple-rainbow sheen, sitting in a small black puddle.
Cell 6 "Crystal": a cluster of three violet hexagonal crystals.
Cell 7 "Diamond": one big brilliant-cut cyan diamond with a white sparkle.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheet): chunky, bold pixel art that still reads clearly when shown at 20×20 px: big simple shapes, strong silhouette, few details. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726), saturated colours, 3-4 tone shading, light from the top-left, one bright specular pixel cluster on metal and gems. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the icon.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: every cell exactly 128×128 px, each icon centred and filling about 80% of its cell, all icons at the same visual size. Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the icon edge touches magenta directly. No frame, no border, no glow, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
