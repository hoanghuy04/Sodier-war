# Máy bắn đá lửa theo cấp

## Cấp 2

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/may-ban-da-l2.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite sheet of the SAME unit as the attached level-1 sheet (a wooden fire catapult (no crew): heavy timber frame on 4 spoked wooden wheels with iron rims, A-frame uprights, a throwing arm with a leather bucket holding a burning tar ball, an iron counterweight, a tar barrel at the front, a small blue team pennant on a pole), now at LEVEL 2 of 3 — "BLACK IRON CATAPULT". Keep the same base (face shape, eyes, body proportions, sprite size, pose set), but the look must EVOLVE DRAMATICALLY and be recognisable at a glance even when the sprite is only 32 px tall. This unit's own colour path is brown WOOD → BLACK IRON → FIRE-RED and GOLD; this level uses the colour in CAPITALS below and must not look like level 1. Each level adds more armour, more horns and spikes and a stronger weapon: clearly cooler and more powerful than the previous level.
LEVEL 2 LOOK:
- Main armour colour: the frame is clad in BLACK riveted iron plates.
- Armour evolution (horns / spikes): spikes on the wheel hubs and on the frame corners.
- Extra details: bigger wheels with iron studs, two tar barrels, iron chains.
- Weapon: a thicker throwing arm and a bigger flaming ball.
Team colour: the cape / tabard / banner / saddle cloth stays royal BLUE (it is recoloured per team); never use blue for the armour itself.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheet exactly): chibi pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, same proportions, outline thickness, palette and shading as the reference swordsman. Big head (about 40% of the body height), large dark eyes with a white highlight, small mouth. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726) around every sprite and between parts. Saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the sprite. Team colour is royal BLUE (cape, tunic, shield field, banners) so it can be recoloured later.

LAYOUT: 8 columns × 6 rows. Columns = the 8 directions below (column 1 = direction 1 … column 8 = direction 8). Rows = poses, one pose per cell:
Row 1 "Idle": loaded and ready, the fire ball flickers.
Row 2 "Move": rolling forward, wheels turning, slight bounce.
Row 3 "Attack": arm swings up and over, the flaming ball launches forward out of the cell.
Row 4 "Hit": shaking with impact marks, a few splinters flying.
Row 5 "Victory": catapult bounces up slightly.
Row 6 "Destroyed": broken and collapsed, a wheel off, small fire and smoke.

DIRECTIONS, always in this order: 1 down (facing the viewer), 2 down-right, 3 right, 4 up-right, 5 up, 6 up-left, 7 left, 8 down-left.
- 1 down: full front view.
- 2 down-right / 8 down-left: three-quarter FRONT view.
- 3 right / 7 left: clean side profile.
- 4 up-right / 6 up-left: three-quarter BACK view: we see the back of the head, the back and the cape; the face is NOT visible.
- 5 up: full BACK view, no face at all.
The weapon stays in the RIGHT hand and the shield/off-hand item in the LEFT hand in every direction. Every piece of gear (cape, plume, quiver, shield…) is present in every direction.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: every cell exactly the same size (160×160 px), the catapult centred, feet (or the lowest point) on the same baseline in every cell, identical scale and design in every cell. Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground shadow, no glow, no halo, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
