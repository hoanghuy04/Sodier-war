# Sư thứu: cấp 3, 4, 5 (Thành trì 6–8)

Tiếp theo bộ cấp 1–2 đã có. Theo hệ chung với công trình: cấp mở ở **TH6 = nước**, **TH7 = dung nham**, **TH8 = sấm sét (tím)**. Màu khác hẳn các cấp cũ, giáp / sừng / vũ khí đồ sộ hơn mỗi cấp.

**Gửi kèm 3 ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png` (Kiếm sĩ mẫu), sheet cấp 1 (`../army/sprite-pixels/su-thuu.png`) và **sheet cấp cao nhất đã có** của chính lính này (để giữ mặt, dáng, cỡ). Gửi lại dạng **File** PNG.

## Cấp 3 (nước)

Lưu thành `sprite-pixels/su-thuu-l3.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite sheet of the SAME unit as the attached level-1 sheet and its highest attached level (a flying griffin with a rider: white eagle head and chest feathers, golden hooked beak, large brown wings with darker tips, tawny lion body, legs and tufted tail. Rider: rounded steel kettle helmet with a blue plume, blue tunic, blue cape, holding a spear in the right hand. It hovers above the ground), now at LEVEL 3 of 8 — "TIDE". Keep the same base (face shape, eyes, body proportions, sprite size, pose set), but the look must EVOLVE DRAMATICALLY and be recognisable at a glance even when the sprite is only 32 px tall. This unit's own colour path is BROWN → SNOW-WHITE and GOLD; this level uses the colour in CAPITALS below and must not look like level 1. Each level adds more armour, more horns and spikes and a stronger weapon: clearly cooler and more powerful than the previous level.
LEVEL 3 LOOK (TIDE, element: WATER), clearly more powerful and imposing than every lower level:
- Main armour colour: deep TEAL and aquamarine scale-plate shaped like fish scales, with mother-of-pearl trims and small coral spikes.
- Hair: sea-green hair with aqua highlights.
- Armour evolution (horns / spikes): a helmet / headpiece crest shaped like a fin or a breaking wave, small branching CORAL horns, seashell-shaped pauldrons.
- Mount / creature / machine: its body / hide turns sea-green and teal with white foamy mane or feather tips, small fins on the legs or wings, a mother-of-pearl armour plate.
- Extra details: pearl ornaments, a thin ribbon of glowing aqua water swirling around the body and a few water droplets (solid pixels).
- Weapon: the rider's spear made of polished aquamarine crystal with silver edges, dripping water drops, bigger and more ornate than before.
Do NOT reuse the colours of the earlier levels of this unit. Team colour: the cape / tabard / banner / saddle cloth stays royal BLUE (it is recoloured per team); never use blue for the armour itself.

STYLE (match the attached reference sheet exactly): chibi pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view RPG/RTS, same proportions, outline thickness, palette and shading as the reference swordsman. Big head (about 40% of the body height), large dark eyes with a white highlight, small mouth. Crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726) around every sprite and between parts. Saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the sprite. Team colour is royal BLUE (cape, tunic, shield field, banners) so it can be recoloured later.

LAYOUT: 8 columns × 6 rows. Columns = the 8 directions below (column 1 = direction 1 … column 8 = direction 8). Rows = poses, one pose per cell:
Row 1 "Hover": hovering in place, wings beating slowly.
Row 2 "Fly": flying forward, wings in a strong down-stroke.
Row 3 "Attack": talons strike forward and the rider thrusts the spear, white sparkle.
Row 4 "Hit": flinching backwards in the air, impact marks.
Row 5 "Victory": griffin screeches, rider raises the spear.
Row 6 "Die": crashed on the ground, wings limp, rider thrown off.

DIRECTIONS, always in this order: 1 down (facing the viewer), 2 down-right, 3 right, 4 up-right, 5 up, 6 up-left, 7 left, 8 down-left.
- 1 down: full front view.
- 2 down-right / 8 down-left: three-quarter FRONT view.
- 3 right / 7 left: clean side profile.
- 4 up-right / 6 up-left: three-quarter BACK view: we see the back of the head, the back and the cape; the face is NOT visible.
- 5 up: full BACK view, no face at all.
The weapon stays in the RIGHT hand and the shield/off-hand item in the LEFT hand in every direction. Every piece of gear (cape, plume, quiver, shield…) is present in every direction.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: every cell exactly the same size (160×160 px), the flying creature centred, feet (or the lowest point) on the same baseline in every cell, identical scale and design in every cell. Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the sprite edge touches magenta directly. No ground shadow, no glow, no halo, no border, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
