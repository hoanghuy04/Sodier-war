# Mẫu vẽ 30 icon trang bị

6 ô trang bị × 5 độ hiếm. Mỗi icon là một món đồ nhìn nghiêng 3/4 như icon trong túi đồ RPG. Vẽ **1 ảnh**: 6 cột × 5 hàng. Tên các món lấy đúng tên trong game.

**Gửi kèm ảnh mẫu:** `../army/sprite-pixels/1.png`. Lưu thành `items/sprite-pixels/trang-bi.png`. Gửi dạng **File** (PNG) để nét nhất.

## Bước 1

```text
Pixel art icon sheet of 30 RPG equipment items, 6 columns × 5 rows, one item per cell, each item seen at a 3/4 angle like an inventory icon.

Columns (equipment slot):
Column 1 "Weapon" (a sword or blade).
Column 2 "Helmet".
Column 3 "Armour" (a chest piece).
Column 4 "Boots".
Column 5 "Ring".
Column 6 "Amulet" (a pendant on a cord or chain).

Rows (rarity, the item gets more ornate and precious row by row, keeping the same slot shape):
Row 1 "Common" (grey-white): plain iron and leather. Weapon "Iron Sword", Helmet "Leather Cap", Armour "Padded Vest", Boots "Straw Sandals", Ring "Copper Ring", Amulet "Wooden Charm".
Row 2 "Uncommon" (green accents): steel and good leather. Weapon "Steel Sword", Helmet "Iron Helm", Armour "Chainmail", Boots "Leather Boots", Ring "Silver Ring", Amulet "Ivory Charm".
Row 3 "Rare" (blue accents): polished steel with sapphires. Weapon "Sapphire Blade", Helmet "Feathered Helm", Armour "Steel Scale Armour", Boots "Wind Boots" (with small wing ornaments), Ring "Sapphire Ring", Amulet "Moon Amulet".
Row 4 "Epic" (purple accents): dark obsidian metal with purple glow. Weapon "Obsidian Sword", Helmet "Demon-horn Helm", Armour "Tar-black Plate", Boots "Scorpion Boots", Ring "Blood-ruby Ring", Amulet "Star Amulet".
Row 5 "Legendary" (orange-gold accents): gold, diamonds and dragon scales, the most magnificent. Weapon "Diamond Dragon Sword", Helmet "Crystal Crown", Armour "Dragon Scale Armour", Boots "Swift God Boots", Ring "Bandit King's Ring", Amulet "Golem Heart" (a glowing cyan diamond heart).

STYLE (match the attached reference sheet): chunky pixel art, crisp 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1726), saturated colours, 4-5 tone shading, light from the top-left, bright specular pixels on metal and gems. Strict pixel grid, every pixel square and sharp: NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO soft glow, NO gradient outside the item.

CELLS & BACKGROUND: every cell exactly 128×128 px, each item centred and filling about 75% of its cell, the same scale for the same slot in every row. Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere; the item edge touches magenta directly. No frame, no rarity border, no glow, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers.
```
